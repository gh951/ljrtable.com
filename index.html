Core Technology · Zero Power

We don't create energy. We reallocate it.

An ornamental fish tank already runs a 20W circulation motor. As a waterwheel grinds grain with falling water, LJR takes the potential energy those 20 watts have already created — and cleans, humidifies and deodorizes the room with no power of its own.

Core Technology · Zero Cleaning

No one scrubs it. The current and the roots do the work.

Waste does settle. The point is that a person never has to remove it. The dual-tank flow carries it out of the inner tank, the roots of the vegetables and flowers above take up and purify the nitrogen, and seven filtration stages return clean water to the ornamental fish. Four years in a real home, with no UV sterilizer and no water change.
