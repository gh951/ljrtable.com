Zero Power

No electricity of its own.

A fish tank already runs a 20W circulation motor. LJR adds nothing to it. As a waterwheel grinds grain with falling water, LJR takes the potential energy those 20 watts have already created and brings it indoors — a four-sided water film that cleans the air.

Zero Cleaning

Nothing settles, so nothing needs scrubbing.

A rising vortex lifts waste before it can settle on the glass. Seven filtration stages convert it to nitrate, and the plant roots above take it as food. Four years in a real home, with no UV sterilizer and no water change.
